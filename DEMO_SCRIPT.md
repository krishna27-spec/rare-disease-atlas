# Demo script

Record the **deployed** app (not localhost). Ubuntu: press **Print Screen**, pick the video-camera icon, choose the browser window, record. (Or OBS Studio.) Do a dry run first; the "What to do next" tab calls the LLM and may take a few seconds the first time, so open it once before recording.

## 1-minute walkthrough (about 150 words)

**(0-10 s)** "Maria's child has MPS IIIC, a rare, untreated brain disease. Very few families, very little data. Where does she start?"

**(10-25 s)** *Type "Sanfilippo C".* "The Atlas ranks the diseases most like hers. MPS IIIA and IIIB share the heparan sulfate degradation pathway and rare symptoms." *Open one 'evidence' box.* "Every link shows its source, date, confidence, and for text-mined facts the exact quote from the paper."

**(25-40 s)** *Open 'What exists'.* "Natural history studies, including two already registered for MPS IIIC, and investigators who already work across these diseases."

**(40-52 s)** *Open 'What to do next'.* "Plain-language next steps, each citing the evidence behind it, plus what an expert must check first."

**(52-60 s)** *Open '10× idea'.* "Instead of building a study from zero, reuse what sister diseases built. Real study durations from ClinicalTrials.gov, no invented numbers, and the evidence tab says what we could not find."

## Team video outline (2-3 min)
1. The problem: rare disease families work in silos (20 s).
2. Live demo of the Maria journey (60 s).
3. How the evidence is kept honest: three evidence types, quote check, dropped-fact counts on the "About the evidence" tab (40 s).
4. The "no supported route" screen: search a disease we don't cover (15 s).
5. Limits and next steps: only part of the abstracts read so far; scale up with the molab GPU notebook; Claude reviewer; more diseases. Mention the cross-correction question only as **future work, not a finding** (20 s).
